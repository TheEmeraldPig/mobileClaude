# Biote privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Biote is a game by TheEmeraldPig. This policy explains what happens to your
information when you play it.

## What the game stores

- **Your save** (money, biotes, upgrades, cards, quests and settings) is kept on
  your device. If Android backup is on, Android may back it up to your Google
  account, as it does for other apps. We never receive it.
- **No accounts.** Biote has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email,
  location, contacts or any other personal details.
- **Deleting your data:** Settings > RESET SAVE deletes your save. Uninstalling
  the game deletes it too.

## Ads and purchases

If a version of Biote shows ads or sells items:

- **Ads** are rewarded ads only: they play only when you tap a button that offers
  a reward, and the No Ads purchase removes them. Ads are provided by Google
  AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and
  information about the ads you see, under
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Where the law
  requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the game asks for your consent
  first, and Settings > PRIVACY > AD CHOICES lets you change your answer. You
  can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
- **Purchases** are handled by Google Play Billing. We get a record that an item
  was bought, never your payment details.

## Random items

The Card Pack, and the card in the Starter Pack, give a random card. The shop
shows the current chance of each card rarity before you buy.

## Children

Biote is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.
